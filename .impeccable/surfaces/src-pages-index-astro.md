# Home page (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: anyone who shops together (family, partners, flatmates) arriving from a recommendation. Job: understand in seconds, then open the web app or download the app (site is presented as launched). Proof: the real app drawn faithfully in HTML, public source on GitHub, an Android APK. No testimonials, counts or store links are invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A household is a transit network. Each person is a station, the list is the line, an item added at one stop arrives at every other in the same second. Refuses the app-landing arrangement (headline, phone, row of feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: Beck/Vignelli diagram. Cool paper ground #e8ede7 with a faint map grid, ink #0e2a1d, four enamel route colours (Home green #4d8c65, Flat tomato #ee7550, Parents honey #edbe6e, Trip blue #3f7fbf). Lines run only at 0/45/90 degrees on one strict grid; stations are ticks and rings; labels are Archivo caps; buttons are enamel station plaques. Presence is the only unprompted motion: stations light when someone is online or adds something.

STORY: Visitor sees one list reach everyone at once, understands why a group chat fails, trusts it works offline and in the shop, then opens the web app or downloads the app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the headline "Everyone you shop with, on one list." in large Archivo, two plaques under it (Open the web app, Download the app). Right, a live diagram: four coloured lines from the edges converge on the real app phone, rising from the bottom edge; a milk pulse travels one line, the phone's toast reads "Sam added Milk", other stations light. Header stays a slim line with station-style nav.
